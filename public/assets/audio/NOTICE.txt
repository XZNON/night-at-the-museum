The WAV files in this folder are original deterministic procedural audio created
for The Last Curator. No external recordings, samples or compositions were used.
The sources are scripts/make-audio.py and, for the Sketch's rock/metallic
loop sketch.wav, scripts/make-sketch-music.py, and for the menu and interface
sounds ui-*.wav, scripts/make-ui-audio.py, and for the museum footsteps
step-*.wav and room tone room.wav, scripts/make-museum-audio.py; provenance and hashes are recorded in
asset-sources/audio-manifest.json. They may be used and distributed with the game.
