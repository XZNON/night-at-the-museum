The Last Curator — Royal Supper study

DreamLayer generated the approved masterpiece, banquet and restorer references,
the player cutout, banquet scenery and bread source. Runtime player poses,
background crops, bread front panels, matching pear and aligned restoration
variants were prepared locally. Existing approved references are preserved.

After repeated DreamLayer upstream 503 failures, the user explicitly authorized
OpenAI ImageGen for the remaining required Royal Supper props on 2026-10-06.
Three reference-guided ImageGen sheets supplied basket, butter, crumb, grape,
jelly, cake, platter, goblet, cover terrine, wax, fork, fan, trident holder and
diner portrait. Runtime images are locally cropped/resized from these sheets.
Collision geometry, landing edges, motion, flame/attention cues and gameplay
remain authored code, independent of generated image pixels.

Unfinished Sketch: the toolbox mechanisms, ground, glue, lifts, nails, pickup
and background tools (assets/sketch/) are cut locally from user-approved
DreamLayer reference sheets by scripts/prepare-sketch-skins.py, with no further
generation. Their sizes come from the level data; collision, hazards and
gameplay cues remain authored code.

Detailed sources, prompts, hashes and provider-specific cost evidence are in the
repository's asset-sources/manifest.json. Known delivered DreamLayer usage is
seven reference plus three production credits. Failed-job costs remain unknown;
ImageGen billing is not exposed and is not counted as DreamLayer credit usage.
