Night at the Museum — art provenance

DreamLayer generated the approved masterpiece and the player heroine; the
matching pear, the enchanted light and the aligned restoration variants were
prepared locally from them. Existing approved references are preserved.

Royal Supper (cartoon rework, 2026-10-09, on the user's request): the banquet
hall backdrop, the museum's banquet painting, the food, tableware, candles,
fan, checkpoint flags and the watchful diner's three poses (eating, turning,
looking) are cut locally from DreamLayer reference sheets by
scripts/prepare-supper-cartoon.py. The earlier M3 Supper art (DreamLayer
scenery and the props the user authorized from OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-10-06
after repeated DreamLayer 503 failures) is no longer used at runtime; it is
kept in the repository under asset-sources/production/supper-m3-runtime/.
Collision geometry, landing edges, motion, flame/attention cues and gameplay
remain authored code, independent of generated image pixels.

Unfinished Sketch: the toolbox mechanisms, ground, glue, lifts, nails, pickup
and background tools (assets/sketch/) are cut locally from user-approved
DreamLayer reference sheets by scripts/prepare-sketch-skins.py. The toolbox
backdrop, torch and museum garage painting were generated with DreamLayer for
the art pass on 2026-10-08 and prepared by the same script. Their sizes come
from the level data; collision, hazards and gameplay cues remain authored code.

Detailed sources, prompts, hashes and provider-specific credit evidence are in
the repository's asset-sources/manifest.json. ImageGen billing is not exposed
and is not counted as DreamLayer credit usage.

Audio is original synthesized work; see audio/NOTICE.txt. Software dependency
notices are in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.
