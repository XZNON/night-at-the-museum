A rich, colourful cartoon background painting for a modern 2.5D side-view platformer, in a playful animated-film style like a hand-inked storybook cartoon: the inside of a giant open red metal toolbox, as if the viewer were a tiny person living inside it, looking straight at its back wall. The whole picture is enclosed by the toolbox: thick riveted red metal side walls at the far left and far right edges, the box floor along the bottom, and at the very top the inside of the open lid with its hinge and a warm golden light glowing down from outside. No sky and no outside world anywhere. The back wall is warm painted metal with rounded rivets, soft dents, cartoon scuffs, peeling stickers and pencil measurement marks, and it changes colour in three wide horizontal zones from bottom to top: warm honey-yellow at the bottom, sky-blue in the middle and rose-pink at the top, meeting in clean soft colour changes with no shelves, beams, rails or ledges across the middle of the picture. Crowded only along the left and right edges and along the very bottom, many oversized colourful tools lean, hang and stack: giant screwdrivers with coral-red and yellow handles, plum spanners, a claw hammer, pliers, a hand saw, a spirit level, clamps, paint tins with drips, masking tape, coils of wire, jars of screws, nuts and bolts. Several of these background tools are alive and grumpy, with cartoon angry eyes and frowns. The wide centre of every zone stays open, calm and softer so small foreground platforms and a little character remain readable on top. Bright cheerful saturated colours, slightly lower in contrast than foreground props. Clean dark plum ink outlines a little thinner than foreground art, broad flat colour areas, simple cel shading, a few loose light pencil construction lines like an unfinished drawing. No realism, no metal reflections, no grain, no photographic texture. No people, no text, no brand names, no readable labels, no signature.
