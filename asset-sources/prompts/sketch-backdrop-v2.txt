A rich, colourful cartoon background painting for a modern 2.5D side-view platformer, in a playful animated-film style like a hand-inked storybook cartoon: looking straight on at the back wall inside a giant open toolbox, as if the viewer were a tiny person living inside it. Perfectly flat, front-facing view of the back wall with no slanted side walls and no top-down perspective. The wall is warm painted metal with rounded rivets, soft dents, cartoon scuffs, peeling stickers and pencil measurement marks. It is divided into three wide horizontal bands from bottom to top: a cosy warm cream and honey lower band, a soft sky-blue middle band, and a rose-pink and golden upper band. Each band is crowded around its edges with many oversized colourful tools and toolbox clutter leaning, hanging and stacked in the background: giant screwdrivers with coral-red and yellow handles, big plum spanners, a claw hammer, pliers, a hand saw, a tape measure, paint tins with drips, a roll of masking tape, carpenter's pencils, paintbrushes, a spirit level, clamps, coils of wire, jars of screws and nuts, sandpaper sheets and a few loose bolts. The centre of each band stays calmer and softer so small foreground platforms and a little character remain readable on top. Very soft, light tray dividers between the bands with no dark lines, reading as distant background and never as ledges. At the very top the underside of the open toolbox lid and a warm golden light glow down over the upper band. Bright cheerful harmonious colours, but slightly softened and lower in contrast than foreground props, as a background. Clean dark-plum ink outlines a little thinner than foreground art, broad flat colour areas, simple cel shading, a few loose light pencil construction lines like an unfinished drawing. No realism, no metal reflections, no grain, no photographic texture, no semi-realism. No characters, no text, no brand names, no readable labels, no signature.
