A finished cartoon painting for a museum wall, in a playful animated-film style like a hand-inked storybook cartoon, matching a colourful toolbox platformer: the inside of a cosy garage at night. A rounded friendly vintage car in coral red, seen from the side, parked on the left; warm cluttered shelves, a pegboard of tools, paint tins and a coiled hose in the background. On the workbench at the right stands a big closed red metal cantilever toolbox, its lid lifted just a crack, and a magical glowing light is peeking out of the gap: soft pale gold and cyan rays and a few sparkles spill out over the bench and the car, the brightest thing in the picture. The rest of the garage is in warm dusky violets and blues so the glow stands out. The edges of the painting fade into loose pencil sketch lines and unfinished outlines on cream paper, as if the picture is not quite finished. Bold clean dark plum ink outlines, broad flat colour areas, simple cel shading. No realism, no semi-realism, no photographic texture, no grain. No people, no characters, no faces, no text, no brand names, no number plates, no readable labels, no signature, no frame.
