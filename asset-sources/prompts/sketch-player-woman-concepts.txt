A character concept sheet for the heroine of The Last Curator, a browser platformer about a museum restorer who jumps inside paintings, from a royal candlelit banquet painting to a bright cartoon toolbox. Stylised animated-film look: clear animation-inspired shapes, simplified two-tone shading, light hand-painted texture, clean dark outlines, no realism. Three different design options for the same heroine, an adult woman, standing side by side with generous spacing, all the same height, full body, each in a strict side profile facing right, neutral standing pose, feet on one horizontal baseline, hands empty, compact friendly proportions with a slightly large head so she reads at about 70 pixels tall, a strong simple silhouette and a light face and one bright accent that stand out on both dark and bright backgrounds. Option one: a museum art restorer with rolled-up sleeves, a long deep-teal work smock over a cream blouse, dark hair in a messy bun with a paintbrush stuck through it, a small brass magnifier loupe on a headband, a little mustard satchel and dark ankle boots. Option two: an adventurous curator in a fitted plum-burgundy long coat, a cream scarf, a short dark bob, round gold glasses, a small antique-gold satchel and dark boots. Option three: a young conservator in mustard-yellow dungarees over a teal shirt with rolled sleeves, a coral bandana tied in her hair with a ponytail, a tool belt holding brushes and a small hammer, and sturdy boots. Friendly, capable, likeable. Perfectly plain flat warm ivory background. No scenery, no ground shadow, no extra poses, no text, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
