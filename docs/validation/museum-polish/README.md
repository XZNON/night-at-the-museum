# Museum polish — frames, walls, runner, lamps, shimmer, sound (2026-10-08)

On the user's requests in the museum polish session, one change at a time with screenshots, before the DreamLayer jam submission. Everything is code-built or original procedural audio (0 DreamLayer credits; balance unchanged at 54). Save identities and schema, artwork IDs, frame positions, return poses, the raycast/interaction contract and the restoration ambience are unchanged. One gameplay-flow change was requested: the museum opens with the masterpiece first (item 6).

## What changed

All in `src/scenes/museum.ts` unless noted; every new geometry, material and texture goes through the scene's `resources` set and is disposed with the room. None of the decoration is solid or pickable: walls stay the only solids and painting meshes the only raycast targets.

1. **Gilded frames.** Each frame is an antique outer lip, a broad bevelled moulding with soft patina, a dark sight edge, a bright beaded inner bead and four corner bosses (extruded rings; two small canvas textures). Frames hug the painting, so the side frames lost their thick top/bottom bars; plaques sit under the new frame edge.
2. **Walls and floor.** Wainscot on all four walls (dark backing, raised panels about 1.3 m wide, skirting with a cap, a dado rail with a gilt bead at 0.82 m so it runs under the side frames; rail and panels stop around the lower-hanging masterpiece), side plaques on the panelling, a gilt cornice and faint striped wallpaper on the upper walls. Five instanced draw calls for all trim. The floor is a seamless canvas plank texture (narrow boards toward the masterpiece, varied tones, staggered joints, grain).
3. **Carpet runner.** Same size and place as before: red velvet with pile speckle, a faint diamond lattice, a gold diamond border between thin gold lines, and a cream fringe at each end.
4. **Brass picture lamps.** Wall plate, curved arm, half-round hood with a bulb strip; when lit the bulb glows and an additive wash falls on the painting's upper part (paintings are unlit materials, so no real lights were added). Lamps fade on over 0.8 s (instant under reduced motion).
5. **Smooth walking.** The museum camera was moved only in the 60 Hz step, so on a 144 Hz display the view froze on about 60% of frames in an uneven 2/3 cadence while walking. The camera is now interpolated between steps with the loop's alpha, as Royal Supper and the Sketch already were; movement, clamping and the click/E pick still use the exact simulated position. Measured on the user's RTX 3050 laptop at 144 Hz while walking forward: per-frame movement went from `0, 0.058, 0, 0.058, 0, 0, …` to an even `0.024–0.0245`; frame time unchanged (6.9 ms median, 7.1 ms p95, also on HEAD).
6. **Masterpiece first (user decision, see DECISIONS).** On a new game only the masterpiece is lit; Royal Supper and the Sketch are greyed out (a luminance mix plus a dark tint on those two paintings only) under dark lamps. Royal Supper is locked ("Inspect the masterpiece first"; click/E do nothing) and the objective reads "Inspect the masterpiece". Closing the first inspection lights both side lamps, restores Royal Supper's colour and opens it, and sets the objective "Find the golden pear in Royal Supper"; the Sketch stays grey and locked ("Restore the golden pear first") until the pear is restored. "Opened" is never stored: a session flag in `src/main.ts` (cleared by reset/New game) or implied by any owned piece. The debug readout adds `supperOpen` and `next`.
7. **Next-frame shimmer.** The frame to visit next carries an additive glint that sweeps diagonally (1.4 s across, every 3.5 s) over a faint pulse; a still soft glow under reduced motion. Next: masterpiece (new game, or while holding a piece) → Royal Supper → Unfinished Sketch → none when complete; it fades between frames.
8. **Footsteps and room tone.** New `scripts/make-museum-audio.py` (original procedural synthesis, no samples, 0 credits) writes `step-wood-1..4.wav`, `step-carpet-1..3.wav` and a seamless 16 s `room.wav`, recorded in `asset-sources/audio-manifest.json` (additions only; no existing audio file or script output changed). `src/core/audio.ts` owns them with the museum bank only: room tone loops under the museum bed at 0.22 and pauses with it; a footstep plays every 1.1 m actually walked (about three a second; none while pushing into a wall), carpet on the runner and wood elsewhere, a random variant at a slightly varied pitch and level, never while paused.
